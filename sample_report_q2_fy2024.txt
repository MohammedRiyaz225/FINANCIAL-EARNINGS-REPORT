APEX TECHNOLOGIES INC.
Q2 FY2024 EARNINGS REPORT
For the Quarter Ended June 30, 2024

FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS

Revenue and Profitability

Apex Technologies Inc. reported total revenues of $7.68 billion for the second quarter of fiscal year 2024, representing a year-over-year growth of 14.2% compared to $6.73 billion in Q2 FY2023. Growth was supported by cloud services and enterprise software expansion.

Gross profit for Q2 FY2024 was $4.82 billion, reflecting a gross margin of 62.8%. Operating income was $1.61 billion for the quarter, resulting in an operating margin of 21.0%.

Net income for the quarter was $1.38 billion. Diluted earnings per share (EPS) was $2.67, compared to $2.01 per diluted share in the prior-year period.

Free cash flow for Q2 FY2024 was $1.84 billion.

Segment Performance

Cloud Services revenue grew 26.5% year-over-year to $3.52 billion.

Enterprise Software revenue increased 10.1% to $2.61 billion.

Hardware & Devices revenue declined 2.1% to $1.55 billion.

RISK FACTORS

The company noted growing competition and macroeconomic headwinds in enterprise spending. Currency headwinds reduced revenue by approximately $110 million. Supply chain disruptions continued to affect hardware margins.

FORWARD GUIDANCE

For Q3 FY2024, the company expected total revenue of $8.4 billion to $8.7 billion, with EPS guidance of $3.10 to $3.25.

END OF Q2 FY2024 EARNINGS REPORT
